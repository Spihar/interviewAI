Privacy note

InterviewAI is part of a college research project on AI mock interviews. Please read this before you start an interview.

What is stored

  • The text extracted from the resume you upload.
  • Your answers, the questions you were asked, and the scores and feedback given.
  • Timing data, such as how long each AI response and transcription took.
  • Your account ID, so your interviews show on your dashboard.

This data is stored in Supabase for the research project.

How it is used

  • The data is anonymised before analysis.
  • Nothing is published with names or other details that identify you.

Services that process your data

Your resume text and answers are sent to Google Gemini to generate questions and score answers, and voice recordings are sent to Groq for transcription. Each provider processes this data under its own terms. If the paid Gemini API is used, Google does not use prompts to train its models.

Audio

When you answer by voice, the recording is sent for transcription and then deleted. Only the text is kept; the audio is not stored.

Deleting your data

You can ask the project owner to delete your data at any time, and it will be removed.
