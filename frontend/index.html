Before we start

InterviewAI is part of a college research project. By taking part:

  • Your resume text and your answers are stored in Supabase for the research project.
  • The data is anonymised before analysis, and nothing is published with names.
  • Spoken answers are transcribed and the audio is not kept.
  • Your resume text and answers are sent to Google Gemini for questions and scoring, and voice recordings to Groq for transcription; each provider processes them under its own terms.
  • You can ask the project owner to delete your data at any time.

How it works
  1. The interviewer reads each question aloud, then your mic turns on.
  2. Answer out loud. Stop talking for a few seconds, or press Done, and your answer is sent.
  3. Press Pause if you need time to think, or Type instead if you'd rather type.
  4. Scores stay hidden until the end, then you get a full report.
